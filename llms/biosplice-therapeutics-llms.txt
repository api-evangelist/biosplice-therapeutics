# Biosplice Therapeutics

> Privately held, clinical-stage biopharmaceutical company in San Diego, California, founded in
> 2008 by Osman Kibar and known until 2020 as Samumed, LLC. Biosplice develops first-in-class
> small-molecule therapeutics acting on novel components of the Wnt signaling pathway and on the
> regulation of alternative pre-mRNA splicing. Its lead program is lorecivivint, an investigational
> intra-articular knee injection for osteoarthritis with a New Drug Application submitted to the
> U.S. FDA. Neurology, diabetes and oncology programs are developed by its affiliate TenaRx, Inc.

## Status of the API surface

no SDK, no GitHub organization, and no machine-readable contract (OpenAPI, AsyncAPI, GraphQL SDL,
Postman collection or JSON Schema) anywhere on biosplice.com. Contract discovery on 2026-08-07
probed the spec paths (`/openapi.json`, `/openapi.yaml`, `/swagger.json`, `/v1/openapi.json`,

honest zero, not a gap: software is not this company's product.
